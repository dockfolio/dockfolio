# AI Search & GEO Knowledgebase

**Created:** 2026-06-29 (session 27)
**Scope:** How search is shifting from Google to AI answer engines, how to be found and cited by them, and a full audit + action plan for all 32 live content sites on the VM.
**Status:** Living document. Update as engines change (they change fast).

This is the portfolio's single source of truth for being discoverable in the AI era. It exists because the old assumption ("rank #1 on Google = you win") is breaking: a growing share of research now happens inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google's own AI Overviews, where there is no list of ten blue links, only a synthesized answer that cites a handful of sources. If you are not in that citation set, you are invisible to that user, no matter how well you rank.

## The one-paragraph summary

Google still sends ~88% of all referral clicks, so classic SEO is NOT dead and remains the foundation. But AI answer engines are the fastest-growing discovery channel (AI search traffic up ~500% YoY), the traffic they send converts roughly 4x better than organic, and Google's own AI Overviews now appear on ~60% of US queries and are quietly eating the clicks of the very pages they summarize. The winning move in 2026 is not "SEO vs GEO" but a three-layer stack: (1) classic Google SEO, (2) Bing indexing (because ChatGPT pulls ~87% of its citations from Bing), and (3) content structured so any LLM can chunk, extract, and cite it. The portfolio is in surprisingly good shape on layer 3 for a few flagship sites (lohnpruefung.de, abschlusscheck.de, bewerbungsfotos-ai.de are near best-in-class) but has concrete, fixable gaps elsewhere, plus a handful of outright bugs that are actively costing visibility right now. See file 03 for who and what.

## The single highest-leverage finding

**No site in the portfolio is blocking AI crawlers** (good, no own-goals). But almost none are verified-indexed in **Bing**, and Bing indexing is the hard prerequisite for ChatGPT visibility. Submitting every domain to Bing Webmaster Tools + wiring IndexNow is the cheapest, highest-impact action available and applies to all 32 sites at once. See `02-geo-playbook.md` lever 1.
