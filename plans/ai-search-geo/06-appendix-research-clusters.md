## Cluster C — AI/GEO & Global Evergreen Tools

### The central strategic split (read first)
Two kinds of "tool" query exist, and they behave oppositely for a new site:

- **COMPUTE queries** (math/convert): `percentage calculator`, `grams to pounds`, `currency converter`, `BMI`, `tip`, `age`, `time zone`, basic loan math. Google answers these **inline in the SERP widget** and AI engines answer them **in the chat text**. Similarweb: "grams to pounds" = **~100% zero-click**; finance/conversion categories "up to 100%" zero-click. A new site cannot win clicks here even at #1 — the answer never leaves the SERP/chat. **AVOID as primary traffic bet.**
- **ACTION queries** (do-a-thing to a file/string): `compress pdf`, `merge pdf`, `resize image`, `convert png to webp`, `qr code generator`, `password generator`, `json formatter`, `remove background`, `word counter`. Google and ChatGPT **cannot perform the action inline** — they must hand the user to a tool. These keep sending clicks AND are exactly what AI engines cite ("you can use a free tool like…"). **This is the winnable lane.**

Your free browser-side calculator works because it's free + instant. The same mechanic applied to ACTION tools (not COMPUTE tools) is where a new English/global site wins in 2026.

**Demoted (high zero-click):** percentage, currency, unit, age/date, BMI, tip, time-zone — huge volume, near-zero clicks.

### How to win the AI citation for a tool page
- **Instant-answer-in-the-page:** working tool above the fold, no signup, no upload-to-server. Lead with a one-sentence "What this does" in the first 40–60 words (the passage AIs extract).
- **Definition-first block** under the tool: 2–3 sentence definition + tight HowTo step 1/2/3. AIs cite a single liftable passage.
- **Schema:** `SoftwareApplication` (price=0) + `HowTo` + `FAQPage`. Cited: FAQPage → ~3.2× more likely in Google AI Overviews.
- **FAQ answering natural-language ChatGPT queries** ("merge PDFs free without uploading", "is it safe", "max file size", "works on iPhone?").
- **Fact/number density + privacy signals** ("100% browser-side", formats list, size limits) — what AI quotes.
- **One tool = one URL.** Splinter every "convert X to Y" into its own page.
- **Static, fast, real h1, no-dash title.** AI crawlers parse static HTML best — vanilla-JS no-build is an advantage.
- **Distribution:** ChatGPT skews Wikipedia-grade + structured; Perplexity skews Reddit/community. Seed a few honest Reddit/Dev.to mentions for Perplexity; schema+authority for ChatGPT/AIO.

### Reality flags
- AIO coverage volatile: 6.49% (Jan'25) → ~25% (Jul) → 15.69% (Nov'25); 8+ word queries 7× more likely to trigger AIO → build for long tail.
- BrightEdge: ~40% CTR drop on AIO queries; pos-1 CTR erosion ~58% late 2025. Model conservative CTRs even in the ACTION lane.
- 58.5% US / 59.7% EU searches already zero-click (Semrush 2025). Only defense = queries needing to leave SERP to *act*.

**Caveat:** keyword volumes are triangulated estimates (±50% across databases); the relative ranking ACTION ≫ COMPUTE is robust. Pull exact volumes in a real Ahrefs/Semrush seat before committing build effort.

### (c) Verdict — advertiser-gold vs display-only
**Advertiser-gold (lead-gen carries them, display is gravy):**
- **Kredit/Ratenkredit/Umschuldungs-Rechner** → 1.75–1.85% of volume = highest €/session tool in German.
- **Baufinanzierungs-/Tilgungsrechner** → €25–32.50 per *lead* (pays on request — best conversion-to-payout ratio).
- **KFZ-Versicherungs-/Beitragsrechner** → €70/sale, mass-market, annual re-shop = recurring traffic.
- **Strom-/Gas-/Heizkostenrechner** → €20/lead, storno-free, seasonal volume.
- **Tagesgeld-/Zinsrechner + Depot/ETF-Sparplanrechner** → €25–50 CPO, evergreen, fintech bidding hard.

**Display-only (lower ceiling under DE adblock):** generic converters (Einheiten, Währung, Prozent, BMI), math/date calcs, **and Brutto-Netto itself** (huge volume but NO natural paid handoff — a top-of-funnel magnet, not a monetizer). Pregnancy/fertility in between: good display (€12–25) + supplement/clinic affiliate, no high-CPA financial lead.

**Ranking rule:** weight each idea by `(search volume) × (affiliate €/session)`, NOT traffic. A Kreditrechner with 1/10th the traffic can out-earn a generic converter 50:1.

### (d) Competition & defensibility
- **Brutto-Netto/Gehaltsrechner:** *saturated* (smart-rechner.de = 208 calculators, brutto-netto-rechner.info, Finanztip). Only entry = long-tail + speed + AI-citation. Low payout — keep as traffic magnet cross-linking to paid tools.
- **Finance comparison head terms:** *owned by portals* (CHECK24, Verivox, Tarifcheck spend millions on TV). Don't outrank "kfz versicherung vergleich". Opening: be the *calculator/educational* layer (Tilgungsrechner, Restschuld-Rechner, Vorfälligkeitsentschädigungs-Rechner, Effektivzins-Rechner) the portals under-serve, then hand off via *their* affiliate programs — earn their CPA without competing on the comparison SERP.
- **Health/pregnancy:** urbia.de dominates + netdoktor/HiPP/Femibion. Long-tail + better mobile UX + freshness is the only wedge.

**Where a small fast free tool breaks in:** (1) long-tail calculator variants the big sites bundle but don't optimize per-page; (2) freshness/"2026" (DE tax params change yearly — update fast in January); (3) AI-citation/AEO (clean single-purpose pages with explicit formulas get cited; cluttered 200-calc portals are poorly structured for it); (4) UX/speed + affiliate-not-display sidesteps the adblock war entirely.

### (e) The €9.99 funnel angle
Free tools where the answer is *"you might owe/lose a lot of money"* convert to paid; *"here's a number"* tools (BMI, conversion) do not.
- **Free Abfindungs-/Fünftelregelungs-Rechner → paid AbfindungsOptimizer.** Severance = one-time windfall + tax anxiety = textbook €9.99 convert.
- **Free Schenkung-/Erbschaftsteuer-Rechner → paid SchenkungsPlaner.** High asset values, fear of overpaying.
- **Free Brutto-Netto → cross-link hub** feeding higher-intent paid tools, not a direct convert.


## Cluster E — English AI-Cited, Ad-Monetized Content & Tools

**Governing constraint (validated):** US Google is now **68% zero-click** (SparkToro/Similarweb, Jan-Apr 2026, up from 60% in 2024); AI-Overview queries run **~83% zero-click**; Pew: with an AIO present, clickthrough drops to **8%** (from 15%) and **<1%** click links inside the AIO. **88% of AIO-triggered queries are informational** — simple facts earn the citation but lose the view. The monetizable wedge is anything the model **cannot reproduce in chat**: a live recompute from user input, a rendered image, a scrollable/sortable surface, or a downloadable file. Citation is **winnable for new sites** — Domain Authority correlates only r≈0.18 with being cited; topical depth is the lever, and only **38%** of AIO citations come from top-10 organic rankers (down from 76% seven months earlier).
